I

About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at New Mexico State University.

Before joining NMSU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the ECE Department at the University of Arizona. I also served as an affiliate member of the Center for Semiconductor Manufacturing.

I completed my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Davis in 2024, with a dissertation on the small evolution of vision transformers for low-power devices. I hold an M.S. in Computer Engineering from Sharif University of Technology and a B.S. in Computer Engineering from K.N. Toosi University of Technology.
